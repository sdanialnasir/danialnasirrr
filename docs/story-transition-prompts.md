# Level-to-level transition: image prompts for ChatGPT

**The idea:** when the diver swims into the gate at the end of a level, they drop into a hole that leads into a tunnel pipe. A short
cinematic shows the inside of that pipe: the diver climbs out of its mouth and walks toward the open sea, with the next aquarium
visible in the distance. The level results (stars, score, "Continue") sit on top. The film freezes on its last frame until the player
taps Continue. Then the next level opens with the diver popping out of a pipe in the sand, at the bottom-left corner of the aquarium.

**How to use these prompts**
1. In ChatGPT, upload the reference images from `refpack.zip` (diver, a level, the result card, the gate, the logo).
2. Paste the **style block** first, then **one shot prompt** at a time. One image per message gives better results.
3. Ask for each shot twice: **portrait 9:16** (phones, the main target) and **landscape 16:9** (computers).
4. Send me the pictures you like. I'll build the cinematic in the game to match them (3D, animated, not just the still image).

## Style block (paste this first, every time)

> Style reference: the attached images are from my game "Aqua Energizer", a cosy underwater puzzle game. Match them exactly:
> a cute chibi diver child with a big round purple-and-gold helmet with a glass visor and a headlamp, brown hair, big friendly eyes,
> a bright yellow diving suit, pink fins and a small air tank (see ref1_diver.png). Polished 3D cartoon render like a premium mobile game
> (Pixar-like softness, rounded shapes, clean materials, soft global illumination). Underwater palette: cyan and teal water with light rays
> from the surface, warm golden sand, rounded reef-rock in brown/purple tones, colourful corals (pink, purple, orange, blue), glowing red
> energy pearls, a glowing blue gate with gold trim (see ref5_gate.png). Calm, magical, cosy mood — never scary, never gritty, no text
> unless I ask for it. The game's levels are glass aquarium tanks with a dark navy frame and small brass corner caps standing on the
> seabed (see ref2_level_computer.png).

## Shot 1 — Inside the tunnel pipe (the first frame after the gate)

> Shot 1 of a short level-transition cinematic. Camera INSIDE a wide underwater tunnel pipe, looking toward its open mouth. The pipe is
> round, made of old teal-green metal rings with brass bolts and a gold trim band, partly overgrown with soft coral, sea moss and small
> barnacles, a few glowing pearl-like lights set into the rings. Water inside the pipe is a slightly darker teal. At the far end, the round
> opening glows with bright cyan sea light and god rays pour in. The diver from the reference is crawling/swimming toward the opening,
> seen from behind three-quarter, helmet lamp casting a warm beam ahead, small bubbles trailing. Composition: the bright pipe mouth is in
> the upper-middle third, the diver in the lower-middle, plenty of darker frame around the edges (room for a results panel later).

## Shot 2 — Climbing out and walking toward the sea (the freeze frame + results)

> Shot 2 of the same cinematic, this is the final frame where the film pauses. The camera is now just OUTSIDE the pipe mouth, low and
> slightly behind the diver. The diver has climbed out and stands on warm golden sand at the edge of the pipe opening, looking ahead with a
> happy, determined pose (one hand raised as if saying "let's go"), helmet lamp on. Ahead, in the soft-focus distance, the open sea:
> light rays from the surface, corals, gently swaying sea plants, a few small fish, and the NEXT aquarium tank standing on the seabed far
> away (glass tank with navy frame and brass caps, glowing softly, like ref2). Leave the left or right half calm and uncluttered (soft
> water and light) so a results panel can sit there. Gentle depth of field, magical cosy lighting.

Then, for the UI version of the same frame:

> Same image as Shot 2, now with the game's results panel overlaid in the calm area, matching ref4_result_card.png exactly: a glossy navy
> card with a bright blue rim and rounded corners, the level name in small caps at the top, "Level clear!" title in white rounded bold font,
> three big glossy gold stars, the score, two small stat tiles (air left, pearls), a coins row, and one big glossy gold pill button
> "Continue ›" at the bottom, plus small round "Replay" and "Levels" buttons. Make it feel like a premium mobile game end screen.
> Portrait 9:16 version: diver and sea in the top 55%, the card in the bottom 45%.

## Shot 3 — Arrival in the next level (pipe in the sand, bottom-left of the aquarium)

> Shot 3: the start of the next level, seen from the front like ref2_level_computer.png (the whole glass aquarium tank, front-on,
> slight downward tilt). In the BOTTOM-LEFT corner inside the tank, the mouth of the same teal-and-brass tunnel pipe pokes up out of the
> golden sand, slightly tilted, with a little sand spilling off its rim and a puff of bubbles. The diver is popping out of the pipe, half
> out, arms up in a cheerful "ta-da!", sand grains and bubbles bursting around. The rest of the tank shows a normal level: sand blocks,
> rounded reef-rock walls, a red glowing pearl, and the blue gate on the far right. Bright, clear, readable; the pipe is clearly the
> entrance and the gate clearly the exit.

## Shot 4 — The pipe itself (a design sheet so it looks the same everywhere)

> A clean design sheet for one game prop: the "tunnel pipe" that connects the aquariums. Show it from 3 angles (front of the mouth, side,
> three-quarter) on a plain soft-teal background. Round, chunky, friendly cartoon proportions; teal-green metal rings with brass bolts
> and one gold trim band at the mouth; soft coral, sea moss and tiny barnacles growing on it; two or three small glowing pearl-lights in the
> rings. Also show one variation per world: Sunlit Shallows (bright teal, sunny), Coral Caves (violet and magenta tint, glowing corals),
> The Deep (dark navy-teal, cyan bioluminescent spots).
