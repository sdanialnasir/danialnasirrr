## Sound effects: public domain (CC0)
- Kenney (kenney.nl) starter kits (github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-*): footsteps (dig), placement thumps (push, bump, bonk),
  impact (stone landing), tile swap (roll), coin (treasure), enemy sounds (bite, death layer). Slowed, muffled and layered.
- uiSFX 0.4.0 (uisfx.com, audio CC0): 'dreamy' pack (achievement, unlock, success, level-up, complete, bonus, progress-step,
  select, info) and 'soft' pack (blocked, warning, press, undo).
- Bubbles (swim, bubble, the bubble layers in land/air/absorb/gate/win, the death "bloop"): physically modelled (Minnaert resonance).

Before release, double-check the Gravity Sound licence on their channel page (the tracks were taken from an MIT game that
credits them; Gravity Sound publishes under CC BY 4.0). Kevin MacLeod's licence is CC BY 4.0 per incompetech.com.
