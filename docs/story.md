# Aqua Energizer: The Thirty Gates (story v5)

## The story in one breath
Grimtide, a giant eel who drinks light, once took the reef’s brightest pearls as tribute until the Reef Keeper built thirty gates and drove him into the abyss. On the storm night (the intro film) he came back, swallowed the reef’s light, and the Keeper gave all her own light to the Great Lantern to save it. A little diver with a lamp wakes the first gate. Pip names them **Spark**. Gate by gate a ragtag crew forms (a nervous seahorse, a 200-year-old turtle, a pompous crab captain, a showman anglerfish) while Grimtide fights back: he steals the Keeper’s map, strikes down Shelly, and snatches Pip; Captain Clack dies saving him. The mural reveals that Spark wears the Keeper’s own lamp. Freed by light given away, Murk the octopus turns on his master. Grimtide lets them light the Lantern because he plans to drink it full, but thirty gates of light, freely given, burn him down to a harmless worm.

## The cast (voice roles in brackets)
- **Spark** [diver] — the hero. Brave, kind, a bit cheeky. Wears the Reef Keeper’s lamp without knowing it (revealed ch. 23).
- **Pip** [pip] — a tiny seahorse whose glow the storm blew out. Talks fast when nervous. Faint after the map is lost (8–9), captured (24–26), brave at the end.
- **Shelly** [shelly] — a 200-year-old sea turtle. Tells the legend (3), shields everyone and falls (13–14), returns with “two hundred years of anger” (25).
- **Captain Clack** [clack] — leader of the Crack Crab Crew, in a tricorn hat. Loud comic relief who leaps at Grimtide’s tail to save Pip (24) and dies a hero (25).
- **Grimtide** [grimtide] — the villain. Silky, amused, cruel. Watches from beside the tanks from level 11, attacks inside levels, and gets the reef’s revenge (30).
- **Murk** [murk] — a giant shy octopus, the Keeper’s best friend, chained to Grimtide’s service. Asks about Spark’s lamp (16), drops a secret pearl trail (18), freed (26).
- **Lumen** [lumen] — the Deep’s showman anglerfish. **Mira** [mira] — a serene manta. **Pip’s cousins** [cousin]. **Snip & Snap** [snip, snap] — Grimtide’s piranhas.
- **The Reef Keeper** [keeper] — heard in her journal (6) and in the 30 hidden gold coins (one per level): her memories tell her own story.

## How the story is told
- **Films** (after each level): every line has its speaker on screen; the camera cuts to a close-up of whoever talks and they animate as they speak. Characters who aren’t part of a scene’s action ride along (Pip at Spark’s shoulder, Lumen glowing beside, Clack at the feet).
- **Choices** in eight films (1, 4, 8, 11, 14, 19, 25, 27): three answers (brave ⚡ / funny 😄 / kind 💛), 9 s to pick; each has its own reply. The style the player picks most changes Pip’s line in the finale (s30_7_<style>). The film slows while you choose.
- **Interactive beats** in eight films: tap fast, hold, or swipe. The film slows to 15% while you act; success and failure each have their own line, and the story continues either way.
- **Inside the levels:** the level card line, a talk bubble when the level starts / the first pearl goes in / after 24 s / the gate opens, and attacks around the tank (the tank shakes, Grimtide’s tail sweeps over it, his eyes watch, ink drifts by, the lights flicker). Levels 11, 24 and 29 open with a short in-level cutscene (the world waits, letterbox, Grimtide stares in). Each beat plays once per session.
- **When a dive fails**, someone reacts: Pip panics, Snip and Snap gloat, Grimtide mocks (from level 11).
- **Collecting:** the hidden gold coin in each level is the Keeper’s; it plays her memory and the journal keeps them all.
