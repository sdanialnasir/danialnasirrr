## The whole poem, read straight through

> One sunny morning, bright and new,
> Ziggy, Kisha… and Benny too!
>
> Benny scored five stars — hooray!
> Kisha snapped a bug at play.
> "Are we there yet?" Benny asked. Ziggy grinned, "Not long!"
> Benny huffed and puffed along…
> Kisha waved bye, and Ziggy cheered her on!
>
> At the market, kids all cheered, "Hello, Ziggy! Hooray!"
> Past the cakes, Benny scrolled away!
> Kisha gasped, "What a sight! Everybody, smile!"
> Click! A perfect memory.
>
> Eyes on his screen… SPLASH! A puddle!
> Poor Benny grumbled, soaked and blue.
> Kisha laughed — click! — "I thought you knew!"
>
> Tweet! Birds on Ziggy — click, click!
> Benny wished for a view up high…
> then shrugged, "Never mind," and scrolled on by.
> But tall Ziggy had the best view in town,
> with a birdie on top, looking all the way down!
>
> Pitter-patter… down came the rain!
> A rainbow! Kisha's camera clicked again!
> "Has it stopped?" Benny peeked, then back to his screen.
> "Let's go!" smiled Ziggy, and off went the team!
>
> Kids waved, "Hi, Ziggy!" He waved hello,
> and Kisha snapped them in a row.
> "Good day so far?" Benny asked. "The best!" Ziggy beamed.
>
> That night, Kisha's album shone so bright…
> the whole day lived in every sight!
> Then Benny's tablet went dark — battery dead!
> "Even screens get tired," Kisha said.
> He stared at the photos: "Did all that happen today?"
> He'd held his screen all day… and missed it all along the way.
> "Tomorrow?" Ziggy winked. "Something worth a snap!"
>
> Now Captain Cyber Champ asks you:
> what zoomed by that you nearly missed too?
> Put it down, look up — the good stuff's right there to see.
> Cyber Champions… that's you and me!

**The story arc:** Benny chases stars on a screen and walks past cakes, puddles, birds and a rainbow. Kisha catches all of it in photos. When his battery dies, the album shows him the day he missed, and Captain Cyber Champ turns it into the lesson.
