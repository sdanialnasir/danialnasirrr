# Benny Spent ONE Day — Rhyming Voiceover Script

All times below were measured from the **audio of the published video** (speech detection + per-line transcription), not from the YouTube transcript. The YouTube transcript runs 1–5 s early in many places.

- **Start** = when the original line starts. Start your clip here.
- **Max** = how long the clip may run before the next line or scene cut. **Never go over it.**
- Every rhyme was written to fit its slot at a relaxed, sing-song pace (about 4 syllables/second).
