How to play

Push every red pearl into the gate, then swim into the gate yourself.

Swim into sand to dig. Pearls and stones only move into open water, so dig the road first.

Stones and pearls fall when nothing holds them up, and roll off round things (left first). One on your head ends the dive, but you can drop them on enemies.

Piranhas, jellyfish and crabs patrol the caves. Touching one ends the dive.

Watch your air. Air capsules give +45 seconds. Finish with more air for more stars: the ★★★ under the timer shows what you would get right now.

Made a mistake? Tap ↶ to undo,Click ↶ or press Z to undo, even after a crash. ⟲ starts over. 💡 shows you the next moves.

Drag anywhere to swim: a short drag steps carefully, a long drag swims fast. Hold the map button to see the whole level.Arrow keys or WASD to swim (or drag with the mouse) · Z undo · R restart · P pause · hold M for the map.
