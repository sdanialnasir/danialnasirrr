How to play

Push every red energy ball into the gate, then swim into the gate yourself.

Swim into sand to dig. Balls and stones only move into open water, so dig the road first.

Stones fall when nothing holds them up and roll off round things. One on your head ends the dive. Drop them on enemies.

Piranhas, jellyfish and crabs patrol the caves. Touching one ends the dive.

Watch your air. Bubbles refill it. Finish with more air for more stars: the ★★★ under the timer shows what you'd get right now.

Made a mistake? Tap ↶ (or press Z) to undo moves, even after a crash. ⟲ restarts instantly. A red ! warns you when a stone sits right above you.

Drag anywhere to swim: a short drag steps carefully, a long drag swims fast.
